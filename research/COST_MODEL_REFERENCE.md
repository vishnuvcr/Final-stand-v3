# Cost model reference

Current Paytm Money F&O FAQ states 10 rupees brokerage per unique executed F&O order. Historical Paytm Money pricing has varied by account vintage. Finance Bill 2026 changes option-sale STT to 0.15 percent from 2026-05-01.

Exchange turnover, SEBI turnover and stamp-duty rates must be supplied as dated inputs from official sources. The backtest must not assume missing rates are zero.
